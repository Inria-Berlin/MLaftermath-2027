# NeurIPS 2026 contributions with Berlin-affiliated authors

Candidate list for the next MLaftermath edition, generated 2026-11-10 by the [neurips-berlin](https://github.com/ml-lab-htw/neurips-berlin) scraper. 338 papers and contributions, 156 Berlin-affiliated authors, and 446 authors from companies on the organizers' list (Prior Labs, NVIDIA, Aleph Alpha, DeepL, Helsing, Black Forest Labs). Company authors are listed because of their employer; their own location was not checked.

This folder is not meant for the website: Jekyll skips folders starting with `_`. Everything here comes from public sources: the NeurIPS 2026 virtual site, OpenReview and the papers' first pages. A Berlin affiliation is listed when it was matched with high confidence or checked by hand; uncertain matches are not included until reviewed. Being listed here says nothing about invitations.

Files: `papers.csv` (one row per paper), `berlin_authors.csv` and `company_authors.csv` (one row per person). In the list below, Berlin authors are in bold and company authors in italics.
